Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Effective August 13, 2026 / 2026 年 8 月 13 日生效

1. Accounts and Tracking / 账户与追踪

NeoGauge requires no account and contains no advertising or analytics tracking. / NeoGauge 无需账户，不包含广告或分析追踪。

2. Local Driving Data / 本地驾驶数据

Speed calculations, journey history, and driving records are processed and stored on your device. NeoGauge does not upload journey history. / 速度计算、旅程历史和驾驶记录在设备上处理和保存。NeoGauge 不会上传旅程历史。

3. Optional Online Data / 可选联网数据

Online Data is off by default. After you accept the in-app disclosure and enable it, NeoGauge sends your current approximate or precise coordinates, rounded to about five decimal places, to Open-Meteo for local weather. Requests occur immediately when valid location is available and then at most every 30 minutes while enabled. / 联网数据默认关闭。接受应用内披露并启用后，NeoGauge 会将当前的大致或精确经纬度（约保留 5 位小数）发送给 Open-Meteo 获取当地天气。有有效位置时会立即请求，之后最多每 30 分钟请求一次。

4. Open-Meteo / Open-Meteo 服务

Requests use HTTPS. Open-Meteo also receives normal network information such as your IP address. Its troubleshooting logs may contain IP addresses and geographical coordinates and may be retained for up to 90 days under its Terms and Privacy Policy. / 请求使用 HTTPS。Open-Meteo 也会收到 IP 地址等正常网络信息。根据其条款与隐私政策，故障排查日志可能包含 IP 地址和地理坐标，并可能保留最长 90 天。

5. Your Control / 你的控制权

You can disable Online Data at any time to stop subsequent Open-Meteo requests. / 你可以随时关闭联网数据，以停止后续 Open-Meteo 请求。
